💡 Strategic Recommendations & Target Customer
👤 Ideal Target Customer Profile
  • 💰 Income: Above $75,000 (Highest response rate group)
  • 🎓 Education: PhD or Graduation level
  • 🌐 Behavior: Frequent web visitors (5+ visits/month) and high web purchasers
  • 🛒 Spending: Customers who already have a high Total Spend pattern
  • ⏱️ Recency: Shopped within the last 30 days
🚀 Actionable Campaign Recommendations
  • 1. Priority Email Campaign for PhD & Master's: Target these customers offering early Gold access. Their 15-20% acceptance rate is the highest among education groups.
  • 2. Create a Family Gold Bundle: Address the low family acceptance rate (10.3%) by bundling discounts on Meat & Fruits, which are more relevant to households with children.
  • 3. Win-Back Campaign for High-Recency: Customers >60 days since last purchase are unlikely to accept. Send reactivation discounts to this group (Recency is the #3 predictor).
  • 4. Digital Conversion for In-Store: Offer in-store shoppers a 10% online discount to build digital behavior, which heavily drives membership acceptance.
  • 5. Wine & Meat Bundle: Design the Gold Membership around top categories (Wines & Meat). Position it as a "Gourmet Food Club" rather than a generic discount.
  • 6. VIP Outreach for High Spenders: The top 11% high-spenders are the most likely to accept (Total Spend is #1 predictor). Give them VIP phone calls and free delivery.
